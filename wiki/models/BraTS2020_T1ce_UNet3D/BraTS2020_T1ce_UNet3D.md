## 설명
Brain tumor segmentation model. Takes a T1 contrast-enhanced (T1ce) NIfTI brain MRI and returns axial-slice overlay images segmenting three sub-regions: Whole Tumor (WT), Tumor Core (TC), and Enhancing Tumor (ET). For glioma / glioblastoma (BraTS).
