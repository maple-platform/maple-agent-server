## Clinical Interpretation

The high probability score (0.87) for pneumonia, combined with a moderate score for infiltration (0.55), suggests the presence of alveolar or interstitial opacities. These findings are clinically consistent with an infectious process, although non-infectious causes of infiltration (such as pulmonary edema or inflammatory lung disease) cannot be entirely ruled out by AI classification alone. The Grad-CAM visualization helps localize the affected lung zones, allowing the clinician to correlate these AI-detected regions with actual radiographic densities.

## Recommendations or Limitations

These results are provided by an AI model and should not be used as a standalone diagnosis. It is recommended to correlate these findings with the patient's clinical symptoms (e.g., fever, productive cough, leukocytosis) and a physical examination. For a more definitive characterization of the lung lesions and to rule out other complexities, a high-resolution CT (HRCT) scan of the chest may be considered if clinically indicated.
