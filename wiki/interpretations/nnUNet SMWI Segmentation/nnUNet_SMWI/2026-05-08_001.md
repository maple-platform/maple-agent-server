## Clinical Interpretation

The segmentation results provide a high-fidelity map of key targets often used in Deep Brain Stimulation (DBS) and the study of Parkinsonian syndromes. The clear separation between the RN and the SN_STN complex allows for a precise anatomical reference in the midbrain. The volumetric delineation of the Putamen is consistent with standard neuroanatomical expectations for this imaging modality.

However, it is important to note that the boundary between the Substantia Nigra (SN) and Subthalamic Nucleus (STN) is grouped into a single "SN_STN" class in this model, meaning the AI does not differentiate between these two distinct but adjacent structures. The accuracy of these boundaries is dependent on the image contrast of the SMWI sequence and should be verified against standard anatomical atlases.

## Recommendations or Limitations

- **Verification**: The segmentation should be reviewed by a neuroradiologist to ensure that the boundaries accurately match the patient's specific anatomy, especially the SN_STN complex.
- **Limitation**: As the model groups SN and STN together, precise targeting for STN-DBS would require additional manual refinement or higher-resolution imaging.
- **Follow-up**: For patients with suspected neurodegenerative diseases, these volumetric results could be compared with normative databases to assess potential atrophy of the basal ganglia or midbrain structures.
